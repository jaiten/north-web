The part other blockers get wrong

An unlock you have to mean.

A blocker you can switch off mid-craving is a to-do list. In North, getting past a block takes three things, and none of them are a click.

1

Stay for the wait

A breathing timer you set yourself, from 10 seconds to 2 minutes. It only counts while North is in front of you — switch tabs or apps and it starts again from zero.

2

Write 20 honest words

A note to your future self about why you need the site, what you'll do there and when you'll leave. Pasting is blocked and keyboard mashing doesn't count.

3

Get a few minutes

Then the site opens, for 5 to 30 minutes and no longer. Every note lands in your unlock journal, where you can read back exactly what you told yourself.

Questions

Answered straight.

What stops me from simply turning it off?
A breathing timer that only counts while you're actually on the page — switch tabs, switch apps, even click away, and it restarts. Then at least 20 honest words to your future self about why you need the site. Pasting is blocked and keyboard mashing doesn't count. You choose the wait when you install North, from 10 seconds to 2 minutes, and shortening it later takes the same challenge.
Is North really free forever?
Yes. Every feature, for everyone, always. No trial, no premium tier, and no feature caps arriving later. It runs on optional tips, and that's the whole model.
Can I still watch the reel a friend sent me?
Yes. Opened from a DM, another app or a pasted link, that single item plays for five minutes. Swiping to the next one is blocked, and you get at most one new pass every ten minutes.
What if I turn something on by mistake?
You get a short free window to switch any protection straight back off, and 30 seconds to remove a site you just added. The four messages-only modes go further: each carries a single free reversal with no timer at all. Everything else — turning North off, strict mode, short videos, your blocklist — takes the full wait and the challenge.
Is my data private?
Completely. No account, no analytics, no network calls. Everything North knows lives in your browser's local storage, and uninstalling takes it with you.
What is lockdown mode?
Exam-week mode. You choose a short allowlist — your documents, your course portal — set a duration of up to 24 hours, and everything else stops loading. There is deliberately no off switch: not the challenge, not a setting, nothing. It ends when the clock ends.
Which browsers does it work on?
Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and other Chromium browsers, plus Firefox. One extension, the same features everywhere. Allow it in Incognito and private windows so the blocks hold there too; North will remind you.
