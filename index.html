05 / A few things to know

Good questions.
Plain answers.

Something else on your mind?
Tell us about it ↗

What does North actually block?
YouTube Shorts, Instagram and Facebook Reels, Snapchat Spotlight, and TikTok. You can also block entire sites, set schedules and daily limits, or block pages by keyword. Messages-only modes keep your inbox available on Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook and X.
What stops me from just turning it off?
To weaken a protection, you first wait on the page for a duration you choose, from 10 seconds to 2 minutes. Leaving the page restarts the timer. Then you write at least 20 words about why you need access. Pasting and keyboard mashing don’t count. Shortening the wait takes the same challenge. You can still uninstall the extension; North is a boundary you choose for yourself.
Can I watch a reel a friend sends me?
Yes. A single item opened from a DM, another app or a pasted link plays for five minutes. Swiping to the next video is blocked. You get at most one new pass every ten minutes. Short-form feeds themselves have no unlock path.
Is it really free forever?
Yes. Every feature, for everyone. No trial, premium tier or feature caps arriving later. Optional tips support North. If you never leave one, nothing changes.
Where does my data go?
The extension stores your settings, journal and usage data locally in your browser. No account, no extension analytics, no server. Uninstalling the extension removes its local data.
Which browsers can I use?
Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and other Chromium browsers, plus Firefox. Allow North in Incognito or private windows if you want your blocks to work there too.
What if I switch something on by mistake?
There’s a short free window to reverse a new protection, and 30 seconds to remove a site you just added. Each messages-only mode also has one free reversal. After that, weakening a protection takes the wait and writing challenge.
How is lockdown different from a focus session?
A focus session blocks the sites on your list until the session ends. Lockdown goes further: only the sites on your allowlist can load. Neither has an unlock path while it’s running. Choose the duration before you begin.
