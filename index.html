Questions, answered straight.

Is North really free forever?

Yes. Every feature, for everyone, always. No trial, no premium tier, and no feature caps arriving later. It runs on optional tips, and that's the whole model.

Does it block YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels?

Yes, as a category: hidden in feeds, unreachable by URL, never unlockable. TikTok can be blocked entirely with one switch.

Can I still watch the reel a friend sent me?

Yes. Opened from a DM, another app or a pasted link, that single item plays for five minutes. Swiping to the next one is blocked, and you get at most one new pass every ten minutes.

What stops me from simply turning it off?

A breathing timer that only counts while you're actually on the page. Switch tabs, switch apps, even click away, and it restarts. Then you write at least 20 honest words to your future self about why you need the site. No pasting, no keyboard mashing. If you can't fill 20 words, you probably didn't need it. You choose the wait when you install North, from 10 seconds to 2 minutes, and shortening it later takes the same challenge.

What if I turn something on by mistake?

You get a short free window to switch any protection straight back off, and 30 seconds to remove a site you just added. The four messages-only modes go further: each one carries a single free reversal with no timer at all, because those are easy to accept before you know what they do. Everything else — turning North off, strict mode, short videos, your blocklist — takes the full wait and the challenge, first time and every time.

Is my data private?

Completely. No account, no analytics, no network calls. Everything North knows lives in your browser's local storage.

What is lockdown mode?

Exam-week mode. You choose a short allowlist — your documents, your course portal — set a duration of up to 24 hours, and everything else stops loading. There is deliberately no off switch: not the challenge, not a setting, nothing. It ends when the clock ends.

Which browsers does it work on?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and other Chromium browsers, plus Firefox. One extension, the same features everywhere. Allow it in Incognito and private windows so the blocks hold everywhere; North will remind you.
